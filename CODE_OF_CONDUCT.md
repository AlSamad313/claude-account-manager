# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short:

- Be respectful and welcoming, and assume good intent.
- Give and accept critique of work, not of people.
- Harassment, personal attacks and sharing someone's private information are not accepted.

## Reporting

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@AlSamad313](https://github.com/AlSamad313), through GitHub, or use the **Report content** option on the issue or comment. Reports are handled privately. The maintainer may
edit or remove content, or block people who break these rules.
